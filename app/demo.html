Plain summary · Beginner

Jargon buster

Transformer

A model design built mainly on attention.

Like a team where everyone can talk to everyone at once, instead of passing notes down a line.

Attention

Deciding which other words matter most for each word.

Like highlighting the key words before answering a question.

Self-attention

Words in the same sentence paying attention to each other.

In "The cat sat because it was tired", working out that "it" means the cat.

Multi-head attention

Several attention processes side by side, each noticing different relationships.

Like a few friends reading the same text, each looking for something different.

Encoder–decoder

One part understands the input; another produces the output.

Like a listener who understands French and a speaker who says it in English.

Positional encoding

Extra information that tells the model the order of words.

Like page numbers on a shuffled stack of pages.

How the method works

1 · Split into tokens→2 · Add word order→3 · Self-attention→4 · Feed-forward→5 · Decoder attends→6 · Generate output

In the app, the AI writes these steps and code draws the diagram, so the picture is always well-formed. This is a teaching simplification, not the paper's original figure.

The famous equation, in plain words

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QKᵀ / √dₖ) V

In everyday language

Compare what you're looking for (queries) with what's available (keys), turn the match scores into weights, then blend the information (values) using those weights. Dividing by √dₖ keeps the scores from getting too big.

Like a library search

Q is your question, K are the book labels, V is what's inside the books. You read more of the books whose labels best match your question.
