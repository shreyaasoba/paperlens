Why it matters: The “T” in GPT stands for Transformer: most modern AI models build on this architecture.

Limitations

  • Shows results on specific translation tasks, not every task.
  • Does not show that later systems share the same measured performance.
Transformer
A model design built mainly on attention.
💡 Like: A team where everyone can talk to everyone at once, instead of passing notes down a line.
Attention
A way to decide which other words matter most for each word.
💡 Like: Highlighting the key words in a sentence before answering a question about it.
Self-attention
Words in the same sentence paying attention to each other.
💡 Like: In “The cat sat because it was tired”, working out that “it” means the cat.
Multi-head attention
Several attention processes running side by side, each noticing different relationships.
💡 Like: A few friends reading the same text, each looking for something different.
Encoder-decoder
One part understands the input, another produces the output.
💡 Like: A listener who understands French and a speaker who says it in English.
Positional encoding
Extra information telling the model the order of the words.
💡 Like: Page numbers in a shuffled stack of pages.
Recurrence
Processing one step at a time, carrying memory forward.
💡 Like: Reading a book one word at a time without skipping ahead.
